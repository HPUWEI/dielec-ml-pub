[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are pm–vm (r=0.993) and vi–vm (r=0.991). These likely co-vary because pm (A-site mass/related parameter), vi, and vm all scale with the overall unit-cell mass and ionic volume, so heavier or larger A/B-site substitutions tend to move them together.

[Redundancy] pm, vm, vi, and m form a tightly overlapping block (pm–vm 0.993, vi–vm 0.991, vi–pm 0.984, pm–m 0.982, vm–m 0.96), and pd–va (r=-0.806) is another strongly linked pair. This indicates substantial shared statistical information, but removal of any single feature cannot be justified from correlation alone.

[Design Implication] For predicting the dielectric property, a compact informative set could combine one representative from the mass/volume block (e.g., vm or pm) with pd and va, since pd–va (r=-0.806) captures a distinct packing/volume contrast rather than duplicating the pm–vm–vi–m cluster.
