[Design Guidance] The dominance of `pd` and `pv` in both SHAP and importance suggests that compositions occupying certain density/polarizability-volume regions are associated with the target dielectric property in this dataset; this is a model association, not evidence that tuning `pd` or `pv` causally improves the property, and any design hypothesis should be validated experimentally before being treated as a physical mechanism.
