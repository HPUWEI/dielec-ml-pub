[Key Features] The dominant features are `pd` (SHAP 13.61, importance 0.312) and `pv` (SHAP 12.00, importance 0.179), with `blm` a distant third (SHAP 2.73, importance 0.151). In dielectric materials these likely encode B-site/A-site ionic polarizability and volume-related packing descriptors (e.g., `pd`/`pv` as polarizability and volume terms), while `blm` plausibly reflects B-site bond-length/mean-bond character influencing polarizability and lattice stiffness.

[Model Insight] The rankings largely agree at the top, but several features diverge: `via` ranks 4th by SHAP (1.886) yet only 10th by raw importance (0.0105), and `cosd` ranks 6th by SHAP (1.334) versus 11th by importance (0.0083), whereas `com` ranks 4th by importance (0.095) but 9th by SHAP (0.650). This suggests the model's dependence is not purely additive—`pd`/`pv` dominate raw splits while features like `via` and `cosd` contribute mainly through interactions or conditional effects captured by SHAP.

[Design Guidance] The association of high `pd` and `pv` with the target, alongside secondary contributions from `blm`, `com`, and `sm`, suggests a hypothesis worth testing: compositions balancing polarizability-type descriptors (`pd`, `pv`) with structural/bond descriptors (`blm`, `com`) may correlate with the dielectric property in this dataset. This is a model-based association only—not evidence that tuning these features causally improves dielectric performance—and would require experimental or first-principles validation.
