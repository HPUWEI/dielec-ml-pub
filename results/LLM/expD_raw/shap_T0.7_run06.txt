[Design Guidance] The model associates high `pd`/`pv` and low `com` with the dielectric target, so compositions where packing/volume descriptors dominate over the `com` term may be worth prioritizing for screening. This is a model association only—not evidence that tuning `pd` or `pv` causally improves the dielectric property—and should be validated against physical theory and measured data before any design claim.
