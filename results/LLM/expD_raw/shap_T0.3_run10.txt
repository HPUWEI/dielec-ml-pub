[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61) and `pv` (11.99), followed by `blm` (2.73). In dielectric materials, `pd` and `pv` likely represent density- and volume-related descriptors (e.g., packing density and unit-cell/perovskite volume), while `blm` likely captures a B-site bond-length or mass-related term tied to B-site electronegativity/radius.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm` rank 1–3 in both), but they diverge lower down: `via` is 10th by importance (0.010478) yet 4th by SHAP (1.885943), and `cosd` is 11th by importance (0.008339) but 6th by SHAP (1.334034), while `com` is 4th by importance (0.095113) but only 9th by SHAP (0.649655) and `sm` is 5th by importance (0.058921) but 12th by SHAP (0.453476). This suggests the model's split-based importances for `com`/`sm` may reflect frequent but low-magnitude usage, whereas `via`/`cosd` contribute larger-magnitude effects on individual predictions, consistent with interaction or context-dependent dependence not captured by raw importance alone.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high `pd` and `pv` with the largest SHAP contributions, and the 80%-importance set (`pd`, `pv`, `blm`, `com`, `sm`, `Em`) suggests dielectric behavior is jointly linked to density/volume descriptors plus B-site and compositional terms; this is a model association, not evidence that tuning `pd` or `pv` causally improves the dielectric property, and any design hypothesis would require experimental or physics-based validation.
