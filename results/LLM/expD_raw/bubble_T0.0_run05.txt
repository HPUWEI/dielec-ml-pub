[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are pm–vm (r=0.993) and vi–vm (r=0.991), with pm–m (r=0.982) and vm–m (r=0.96) also very high. These likely co-vary because pm (A-site/B-site ionic mass proxy), vm (molar/unit-cell volume), vi (ionic volume), and m (mass) all scale together with the size and identity of the constituent cations in the perovskite-like lattice.

[Redundancy] The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} carries heavily overlapping statistical information, and pd–va (r=-0.806) is a strong inverse pair. This overlap suggests redundancy among these features, but it does not by itself prove that any one feature can be removed without testing its unique contribution to the Gradient Boosting model.

[Design Implication] Given this correlation structure, a compact informative set could combine one representative of the {pm, vm, vi, m} size/mass cluster (e.g., vm) with pd or va to capture the inverse packing/volume contrast, plus chemically distinct descriptors such as the electronegativity and ionization-energy features (emb, eab, epa, ieb) and GII. This would preserve size, mass, and electronic-structure information while limiting collinear duplication for predicting the dielectric property.
