[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61), `pv` (12.00), and `blm` (2.73), with `pd` and `pv` dominating by roughly 4-5x over the next feature. In dielectric materials, `pd` and `pv` likely represent density-related descriptors (e.g., packing/volume-normalized density terms), while `blm` plausibly encodes a B-site bond-length or electronegativity-related mean consistent with the researcher's B-site descriptors (emb, eab, ieb).

[Design Guidance] The model associates high `pd`/`pv` with the dielectric target and gives `via` and `cosd` larger SHAP influence than their raw importances imply, hinting that composition/volume-related descriptors may interact with density terms; this is a model association only, not evidence that tuning `pd`, `pv`, `via`, or `cosd` will causally improve the dielectric property, which would require controlled synthesis and measurement to establish.
