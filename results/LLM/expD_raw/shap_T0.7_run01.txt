[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (13.6128) and **pv** (11.9977), followed by **blm** (2.7318); **pd** and **pv** likely represent polarization-related descriptors (e.g., polarizability/distortion contributions), while **blm** may capture B-site bond-length or electronegativity-related character in the perovskite/dielectric framework.

[Model Insight] SHAP ranking diverges from raw importance for several features: **via** ranks 4th by SHAP (1.8859) but only 10th by importance (0.0105), and **cosd** ranks 6th by SHAP (1.3340) but 11th by importance (0.0083), whereas **com** and **sm** are high in raw importance (0.0951, 0.0589) but much lower by SHAP (0.6497, 0.4535); this suggests the model’s split-based reliance on **com**/**sm** may partly reflect interactions or correlated pathways, while **via** and **cosd** contribute more consistently to individual predictions.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high **pd** and **pv** with the dielectric target, and **blm** as a secondary association, so compositions/structures varying these descriptors—especially **pd**, **pv**, and **blm**—may be worth prioritizing for model-guided screening; however, this is only a learned association, not evidence that changing **pd**, **pv**, or **blm** will causally improve the dielectric property, and physical validation is required.
