[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61), `pv` (12.00), and `blm` (2.73), with `pd` and `pv` dominating by roughly 4-5x over the next feature. In dielectric materials, `pd` and `pv` likely encode packing/density-related descriptors (e.g., packing density and packing volume fraction), while `blm` plausibly represents a B-site bond-length or mass-related term tied to B-site electronegativity/radius inputs.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm` rank 1-2-3 in both), but diverge below: `via` is 10th by raw importance (0.010478) yet 4th by SHAP (1.886), and `cosd` is 11th by importance (0.008339) but 6th by SHAP (1.334), whereas `com` is 4th by importance (0.095113) but only 9th by SHAP (0.650). This suggests the model's split-based importance overstates `com` (likely used frequently for many small splits) while SHAP reveals `via` and `cosd` carry larger per-instance directional effects, consistent with interaction or context-dependent contributions.

[Design Guidance] The concentration of SHAP mass in `pd` and `pv` (13.61 and 12.00 vs. 2.73 for `blm`) suggests the model associates dielectric response most strongly with packing/density-type descriptors, so compositions or processing routes that shift `pd`/`pv` alongside `blm` may be worth screening. This is a model association only, not evidence that manipulating `pd` or `pv` causally improves the dielectric property; the R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632 and RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395 indicate substantial unexplained variance, so any hypothesis requires physical validation.
