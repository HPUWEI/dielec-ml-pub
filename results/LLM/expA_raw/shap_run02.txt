[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm`), but they diverge for `via` (SHAP rank 4, importance rank 10 at 0.0105) and `com` (importance rank 4 at 0.0951, SHAP rank 9 at 0.6497). [Design Guidance] The model associates high `pd` and `pv` with the dielectric target, and `blm` adds B-site bonding information, so compositions jointly varying these descriptors may be worth prioritizing for screening. This is a model association only—not evidence that changing `pd`, `pv`, or `blm` causally improves the dielectric property—and would require experimental or first-principles validation.
