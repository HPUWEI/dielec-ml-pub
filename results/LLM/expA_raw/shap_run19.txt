[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61), `pv` (12.00), and `blm` (2.73). In dielectric materials, `pd` and `pv` likely represent density-related or polarization/volume descriptors tied to packing and polarizability, while `blm` (B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov) reflects B-site bonding character and covalency.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm` lead both), but they diverge lower down: `via` ranks 4th by SHAP (1.89) yet only 10th by raw importance (0.0105), and `cosd` ranks 6th by SHAP (1.33) versus 11th by importance (0.0083), while `com` ranks 4th by importance (0.0951) but 9th by SHAP (0.65). This suggests `via` and `cosd` contribute strongly through interactions or context-dependent effects rather than large standalone splits, whereas `com`'s high raw importance may partly reflect correlated or redundant signal.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high `pd` and `pv` with the dielectric target, and their dominance alongside `blm` suggests composition/structural descriptors jointly matter more than any single B-site electronegativity term. This is a model association only—not evidence that tuning `pd` or `pv` causally improves dielectric performance—so any design hypothesis should be validated experimentally before being treated as a physical mechanism.
