[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (13.61), **pv** (12.00), and **blm** (2.73), with **pd** and **pv** dominating by roughly 5–10× over the next tier. In dielectric materials, **pd** and **pv** likely encode packing-density and pore/void-volume descriptors (bulk and volumetric), while **blm** plausibly represents a B-site bond-length or mass-related term tied to B-site electronegativity/radius inputs such as emb and arb.

[Model Insight] Raw importance and SHAP rankings largely agree at the top (**pd** 0.312, **pv** 0.179, **blm** 0.151), but they diverge below that: **via** ranks 10th by importance (0.0105) yet 4th by SHAP (1.886), and **cosd** ranks 11th by importance (0.0083) but 6th by SHAP (1.334), while **com** ranks 4th by importance (0.0951) but only 9th by SHAP (0.650) and **sm** ranks 5th by importance (0.0589) but 12th by SHAP (0.453). This suggests the model's split-based importance over-credits **com** and **sm** (possibly used as frequent splitting proxies), whereas **via** and **cosd** carry larger conditional/marginal contributions, consistent with interaction or context-dependent effects not captured by raw gain.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high **pd** and **pv** with the dielectric target, and the 80%-importance set (**pd, pv, blm, com, sm, Em**) suggests compositional/structural descriptors co-vary with it; a cautious hypothesis is that compositions simultaneously achieving favorable packing (**pd**, **pv**) and B-site bonding character (**blm**, **Em**) may be worth prioritizing for screening. This is a model association only—the SHAP and importance values do not establish that tuning **pd**, **pv**, or **blm** causally improves the dielectric property, and the wide CV spread (R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632, RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395) warrants experimental validation before any design claim.
