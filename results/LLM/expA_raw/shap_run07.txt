[Key Features] The dominant features are `pd` (SHAP mean |value| = 13.61, importance = 0.312) and `pv` (SHAP = 12.00, importance = 0.179), followed by `blm` (SHAP = 2.73, importance = 0.151). In dielectric perovskites, `pd` and `pv` plausibly encode A-site/B-site ionic polarizability or polarizability-volume terms that govern dipole response, while `blm` (B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov) reflects B-site bonding covalency and hence polarizability of the BO₆ octahedron.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree on the top three (`pd`, `pv`, `blm`), but they diverge lower down: `via` ranks 4th by SHAP (1.89) yet only 10th by importance (0.010), and `cosd` ranks 6th by SHAP (1.33) versus 11th by importance (0.008), whereas `com` ranks 4th by importance (0.095) but 9th by SHAP (0.650) and `sm` ranks 5th by importance (0.059) but 12th by SHAP (0.453). This suggests `com` and `sm` contribute mainly through interactions or correlated splits that inflate tree-based importance, while `via` and `cosd` carry more consistent, directionally meaningful marginal contributions.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high `pd`/`pv` and elevated `blm` with the dielectric target, so compositions combining large polarizability-volume descriptors with moderate B-site electronegativity are worth prioritizing for screening. This is a model-based association only—not evidence that tuning `pd`, `pv`, or `blm` causally improves the dielectric property—and the wide CV spread (R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632, RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395) warrants experimental validation before any design claim.
