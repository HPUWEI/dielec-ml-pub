[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are pm–vm (r=0.993) and vi–vm (r=0.991). These likely co-vary because pm (A-site-related mass/parameter), vm (unit-cell or molar volume), and vi (ionic volume) all scale with the size and packing of the perovskite lattice, so larger A/B-site ions tend to increase both volume and mass-related descriptors together.

[Redundancy] pm, vm, vi, and m form a tightly overlapping block (pm–vm=0.993, vi–vm=0.991, vi–pm=0.984, pm–m=0.982, vm–m=0.96), and pd–va (r=-0.806) is a separate strongly anti-correlated pair. This indicates substantial shared statistical information among the volume/mass descriptors and between pd and va, but removal of any single feature should not be decided from correlation alone.

[Design Implication] For predicting the dielectric property, a compact informative set could combine one representative from the volume/mass cluster (e.g., vm or pm) with pd and va to retain the negative-covarying structural/electronic contrast, plus distinct descriptors such as the B-site electronegativity/ionization features (blm, blsd, Em, Ev, Esd) and GII that are not part of the strong-correlation blocks.
