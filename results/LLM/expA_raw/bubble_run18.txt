[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are pm–vm (r=0.993) and vi–vm (r=0.991). These likely co-vary because pm (A-site/B-site related mass or molar descriptor), vm (molar volume), and vi (ionic volume) all scale with the size and mass of the constituent ions in the perovskite lattice, so larger/heavier compositions tend to shift all three together.

[Redundancy] The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} shows overlapping statistical information, with pm–m (r=0.982) and vm–m (r=0.96) also very high; pd and va are strongly inversely linked (r=-0.806). This overlap suggests these features are largely redundant in a correlation sense, but removal of any one cannot be justified from correlation alone because each may still contribute unique nonlinear or interaction information to the Gradient Boosting model.

[Design Implication] Given this structure, a compact informative set could pair one representative from the {pm, vm, vi, m} cluster (e.g., vm or pm) with pd or va (but not both, since r=-0.806), and retain the remaining weakly correlated descriptors such as blm, blsd, sm, ssd, com, cosd, Em, Ev, Esd, bvsdm, devvm, via, pa, pv, lsd, asd, d, and GII to capture complementary chemical and structural effects on the dielectric property.
