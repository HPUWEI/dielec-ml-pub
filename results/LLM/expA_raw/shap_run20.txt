[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (13.61), **pv** (12.00), and **blm** (2.73). In dielectric materials, **pd** and **pv** likely represent density-related descriptors (e.g., packing or volumetric density terms), while **blm** (B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov) captures B-site bonding/electronegativity character that influences polarizability and dielectric response.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (**pd**, **pv**, **blm** rank 1–3 in both), but they diverge lower down: **via** is 10th by raw importance (0.0105) yet 4th by SHAP (1.89), and **cosd** is 11th by importance (0.0083) but 6th by SHAP (1.33), whereas **com** is 4th by importance (0.0951) but only 9th by SHAP (0.65) and **sm** is 5th by importance (0.0589) but 12th by SHAP (0.45). This suggests the model's split-based importances for **com** and **sm** may be inflated by frequent but low-magnitude splits or interactions, while **via** and **cosd** carry larger per-instance contributions than their split frequency implies.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high **pd** and **pv** with the dielectric target alongside **blm**, so compositions where these density/volumetric descriptors and B-site electronegativity co-vary favorably are worth prioritizing for screening. This is a model association only—it does not establish that tuning **pd**, **pv**, or **blm** will causally improve the dielectric property, and the wide CV spread (R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632, RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395) warrants experimental validation before any design claim.
