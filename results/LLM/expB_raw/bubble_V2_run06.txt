[Strong Correlations]
The two most strongly correlated pairs are pm–vm (r=0.993) and vi–vm (r=0.991), with vi–pm also extremely high at r=0.984. These likely co-vary because pm (A-site mass-related descriptor), vm (unit-cell/volume-related descriptor), and vi (ionic/volume-related descriptor) all scale together with the overall atomic packing and unit-cell size in perovskite-like dielectrics.

[Redundancy]
The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} shows overlapping statistical information, since pm–vm=0.993, vi–vm=0.991, vi–pm=0.984, pm–m=0.982, and vm–m=0.96, while pd–va is strongly negatively correlated at r=-0.806. These features are statistically redundant with one another, but none can be definitively removed based only on correlation values.

[Design Implication]
For predicting the dielectric property, a compact informative set could combine one representative from the {pm, vm, vi, m} cluster with pd or va to capture the strong negative pd–va relationship (r=-0.806), while retaining chemically distinct descriptors such as blm, blsd, sm, ssd, com, cosd, Em, Ev, Esd, bvsdm, devvm, via, pa, pv, lsd, asd, d, va, and GII to preserve complementary materials-science information.
