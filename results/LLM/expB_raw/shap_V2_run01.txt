[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are pd (13.61), pv (12.00), and blm (2.73), with pd and pv dominating by roughly 5–10× over the next feature. In dielectric materials, pd and pv likely correspond to packing-density and pore-volume descriptors (or related density/volume terms), while blm appears to be a B-site bond-length or bond-valence-related term.

[Model Insight] The raw importance ranking (pd 0.312, pv 0.179, blm 0.151, com 0.095, sm 0.059, Em 0.048) broadly agrees with SHAP at the top, but diverges lower down: via ranks 4th in SHAP (1.89) yet only 10th in raw importance (0.010), and cosd ranks 6th in SHAP (1.33) versus 11th in importance (0.008), whereas com ranks 4th in importance (0.095) but only 9th in SHAP (0.65). This suggests the model relies on via and cosd through interactions that raw split-based importance understates, while com's importance is partly redundant with pd/pv.

[Design Guidance] The association of high pd and pv with the dielectric target suggests that compositions favoring denser packing and controlled pore volume may be worth prioritizing for screening, but this is a model association, not evidence that changing pd or pv causally improves the dielectric property.
