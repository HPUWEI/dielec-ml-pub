[Dominance] Six of the 24 features account for 80% of predictive importance: pd (0.312094), pv (0.178505), blm (0.151432), com (0.095113), sm (0.058921), and Em (0.048412). Importance is highly concentrated, with pd and pv alone contributing 0.490599 (~49%) and the remaining 18 features each falling below 0.012.

[Physical Meaning] pd and pv likely encode packing-density and volume-related descriptors that govern polarizability and dipole response, which directly control dielectric constant and loss. blm, the B-site electronegativity (Matyonov-Batsanov), influences B–O bond covalency and hence ionic/electronic polarizability in perovskite-like dielectrics. com and sm, as compositional and stoichiometric/mean descriptors, capture cation ordering and average chemistry effects that shift dielectric behavior across compositions.
