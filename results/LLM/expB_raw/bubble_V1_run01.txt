[Strong Correlations] The two strongest feature pairs are ('pm', 'vm', r=0.993) and ('vi', 'vm', r=0.991), both involving the molar/volume-type descriptors vm and pm. These likely co-vary because in perovskite-type dielectrics, molar mass and molar volume scale together with B-site cation identity and packing density, so heavier B-site compositions tend to occupy larger unit-cell volumes.

[Redundancy] The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} is highly redundant, with ('vi', 'pm', r=0.984), ('pm', 'm', r=0.982), and ('vm', 'm', r=0.96), and the negative pair ('pd', 'va', r=-0.806) also overlaps in information about packing/volume. Correlation alone does not justify removing any of pm, vm, vi, m, pd, or va, since each may still carry unique nonlinear or interaction signal for the dielectric target.

[Design Implication] Given this structure, a compact informative combination would pair one representative from the redundant volume/mass cluster (e.g., vm or pm) with the negatively correlated packing descriptors pd and va, plus chemically distinct features such as the electronegativity/ionization terms (blm, blsd, Em, Ev, Esd) and GII. This avoids over-weighting the pm–vm–vi–m axis while retaining complementary information for predicting the dielectric property.
