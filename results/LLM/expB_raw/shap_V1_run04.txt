[Key Features] The three most important features by SHAP mean |value| are pd (13.61), pv (12.00), and blm (2.73), which likely reflect packing density, pore volume, and B-site electronegativity (Matyonov-Batsanov scale) respectively in dielectric materials. [Model Insight] The SHAP ranking largely agrees with the raw importance ranking for the top three (pd, pv, blm), but diverges notably for via (SHAP rank 4, importance rank 10) and com (SHAP rank 9, importance rank 4), indicating that via has a strong local effect on predictions despite low global split usage, while com is frequently used for splits but with smaller average impact. This suggests the model relies on pd and pv as primary drivers, with via and com participating in interactions that SHAP captures more sensitively than raw importance.

[Design Guidance] The strong association of pd and pv with the target suggests that compositions or processing routes yielding moderate packing density and pore volume may correlate with favorable dielectric properties, but this is a model association only—not evidence that tuning these features causally enhances performance. Experimental validation is required before treating pd or pv as design levers.
