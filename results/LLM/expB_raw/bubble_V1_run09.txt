[Strong Correlations] The two strongest pairs are ('pm', 'vm', 0.993) and ('vi', 'vm', 0.991), with ('vi', 'pm', 0.984) close behind. These co-vary plausibly because pm (A-site/B-site-related mass or molar parameter), vm (molar volume), and vi (ionic volume) all scale with atomic/ionic size and composition in perovskites, so heavier or larger constituent ions tend to increase both mass and volume together.

[Redundancy] The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} shares heavily overlapping statistical information, as seen in ('pm', 'm', 0.982) and ('vm', 'm', 0.96), and ('pd', 'va', -0.806) indicates a strong inverse relationship between those two features. Correlation alone does not prove that any of pm, vm, vi, or m is removable, since each may still carry unique variance relevant to the dielectric target under the Gradient Boosting model.

[Design Implication] Given this structure, a compact but informative combination would pair one representative of the size/volume cluster (e.g., vm or pm) with pd and va (which are strongly anti-correlated at -0.806) and with chemically distinct descriptors such as the electronegativity/ionization features (emb, eab, epa, ieb) and GII, rather than feeding all of pm, vm, vi, and m simultaneously.
