[Key Features] The three most important features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (13.61), **pv** (12.00), and **blm** (2.73), which likely reflect packing density, pore volume, and B-site electronegativity (Matyonov-Batsanov scale), respectively—properties tied to polarizability and ionic bonding in dielectric perovskites. The next tier, **via** (1.89) and **Ev** (1.40), suggests ionic volume and energy-related terms also contribute meaningfully.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking largely agrees with the raw importance ranking at the top (**pd**, **pv**, **blm** lead both), but diverges notably for **via** (SHAP rank 4 vs. importance rank 10, 0.010478) and **cosd** (SHAP rank 6 vs. importance rank 11, 0.008339), while **com** drops from importance rank 4 (0.095113) to SHAP rank 9 (0.649655). This implies the model relies on **via** and **cosd** through interactions that raw split-based importance understates, whereas **com**'s high importance may reflect frequent but low-magnitude splits.

[Design Guidance] The dominance of **pd** and **pv** (combined SHAP 25.61 of ~35 total) suggests that compositions with intermediate packing density and pore volume may associate with the target dielectric response, but this is a model association, not evidence that tuning these features causally enhances dielectric properties. Experimental validation is needed before treating **pd** or **pv** as design levers.
