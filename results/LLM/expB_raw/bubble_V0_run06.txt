[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are pm–vm (r = 0.993) and vi–vm (r = 0.991), with vi–pm close behind at r = 0.984. These likely co-vary because pm (A-site/B-site mass-related descriptor), vm (molar/unit-cell volume), and vi (ionic volume) all scale with the ionic size and mass of the constituent cations, so heavier, larger ions tend to produce both larger ionic volumes and larger cell volumes.

[Redundancy] The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} carries heavily overlapping statistical information, since pm–m = 0.982 and vm–m = 0.96 in addition to the pm–vm–vi correlations above; the pair pd–va (r = -0.806) is a separate, weaker redundancy. Correlation alone does not establish that any of pm, vm, vi, or m can be removed, because each may still contribute unique non-linear signal to the Gradient Boosting model.

[Design Implication] Given this structure, a compact informative set would pair one representative of the size/volume cluster (e.g., vm or vi) with the distinct packing/volume-ratio axis represented by pd and va, and then add chemically independent descriptors such as the electronegativity and ionization-energy features (blm, blsd, Em, Ev, Esd, GII) that are not entangled in the |r|≥0.5 list. This combination spans ionic size/volume, A–B site packing mismatch, and electronic/chemical bonding character, which are the physically relevant levers for the dielectric response targeted by the R²=0.7472 model.
