[Key Features] The three most important features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61), `pv` (12.00), and `blm` (2.73), with `pd` and `pv` dominating by roughly 4–5× over `blm`. In dielectric materials, `pd` and `pv` likely encode packing-density or polarizability-volume descriptors tied to the perovskite A/B-site sublattices, while `blm` (B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov) reflects B-site bonding covalency and its influence on polarizability.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking largely agrees with the raw importance ranking at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm`), but diverges notably for `via` (SHAP rank 4, 1.89 vs. importance rank 10, 0.0105), `cosd` (SHAP rank 6, 1.33 vs. importance rank 11, 0.0083), and `com` (SHAP rank 9, 0.65 vs. importance rank 4, 0.0951). [Design Guidance] The strong SHAP association of `pd` and `pv` with the target, together with the secondary role of `blm`, suggests that compositions simultaneously optimizing packing/polarizability-volume descriptors and B-site electronegativity may be worth prioritizing in screening. This is a model association only—`pd`, `pv`, and `blm` are correlated descriptors, and the analysis does not establish that modifying any of them will causally enhance the dielectric property.
