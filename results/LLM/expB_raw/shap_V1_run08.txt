[Key Features] The three most important features by SHAP mean |value| are pd (13.612782), pv (11.997661), and blm (2.731782), which likely reflect A-site/B-site ionic polarizability, ionic volume or packing, and B-site electronegativity (Matyonov-Batsanov scale), respectively, all of which influence dielectric polarization and permittivity. The raw importance ranking also places pd (0.312094) and pv (0.178505) first and second, with blm third (0.151432), reinforcing that these three dominate both model splits and prediction magnitude.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking disagrees with the raw importance ranking for via (SHAP 1.885943 vs. importance 0.010478) and cosd (SHAP 1.334034 vs. importance 0.008339), which are far more influential in SHAP than their split-based importance suggests, while com (importance 0.095113 vs. SHAP 0.649655) and sm (importance 0.058921 vs. SHAP 0.453476) show the opposite pattern. This implies the model relies on via and cosd mainly through interactions or context-dependent effects rather than as frequent primary splitters, whereas com and sm contribute more to tree structure than to large-magnitude predictions.

[Design Guidance] Because pd and pv dominate both SHAP (13.612782 and 11.997661) and raw importance (0.312094 and 0.178505), compositions with high values on these descriptors are associated with the model’s higher dielectric predictions, but this is a model association and not evidence that changing pd or pv causally enhances the dielectric property. Any design hypothesis should therefore treat pd and pv as screening variables to prioritize candidates for experimental validation, while recognizing that the reported R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632 and RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395 leave substantial unexplained variance.
