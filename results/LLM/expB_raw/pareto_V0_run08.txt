[Physical Meaning] pd (0.312094) and pv (0.178505) are the two dominant predictors, and their high combined weight suggests dielectric response is governed largely by these two descriptors, plausibly packing-density and volume-related terms that control polarizability per unit cell. blm (0.151432), the B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov term, ranks third, consistent with B-site cation chemistry tuning bond covalency and hence ionic/electronic polarizability. com (0.095113) and sm (0.058921) add further compositional and size-related information, while Em (0.048412) likely captures an energy-scale term tied to polarization response.
