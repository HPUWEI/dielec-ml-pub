[Physical Meaning] pd and pv are the two strongest predictors, plausibly reflecting packing-density and volume-related descriptors that govern polarizability and dipole response in the dielectric lattice. blm, the B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov term, likely captures B-site bonding character and charge distribution, which directly influence ionic and electronic contributions to dielectric behavior. com, a compositional descriptor, may encode stoichiometric or cation-mixing effects that shift the dielectric response across compositions.
