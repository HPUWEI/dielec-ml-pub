[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (13.61), **pv** (12.00), and **blm** (2.73), which likely represent dielectric-related polarizability/volume descriptors at the A- or B-site, unit-cell polarizability/volume terms, and a B-site mass or bonding-related term respectively. These dominate both importance and SHAP, suggesting they encode the primary compositional/structural drivers of the dielectric response in this dataset.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (**pd**, **pv**, **blm**), but diverge lower down: **via** ranks 10th by importance (0.0105) yet 4th by SHAP (1.89), while **com** ranks 4th by importance (0.0951) but 9th by SHAP (0.65), and **sm** ranks 5th by importance (0.0589) but 12th by SHAP (0.45). This suggests the model's split-based importance overstates **com** and **sm** (possibly used in many low-impact splits or interactions), whereas **via** carries larger per-instance directional effects, indicating interaction-dependent or context-sensitive contributions.

[Design Guidance] The co-dominance of **pd** and **pv** with **blm** suggests the model associates dielectric behavior with combined polarizability/volume and B-site bonding descriptors, so compositions balancing these may be worth prioritizing for screening. This is a model association only—not evidence that tuning **pd**, **pv**, or **blm** will causally improve the dielectric property—and given the wide CV spread (R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632, RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395), any such hypothesis requires experimental validation.
