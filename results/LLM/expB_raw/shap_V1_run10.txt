[Key Features] The three most important features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (13.61), **pv** (12.00), and **blm** (2.73), with **pd** and **pv** dominating by roughly 4–5× over **blm**. In dielectric materials, **pd** and **pv** likely encode packing-density and volume-related descriptors that influence polarizability and dipole response, while **blm** (B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov) reflects B-site bonding character and covalency that affect the dielectric constant.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking broadly agrees with the raw importance ranking at the top (**pd** 0.312, **pv** 0.179, **blm** 0.151), but diverges lower down: **via** ranks 4th in SHAP (1.89) yet only 10th in raw importance (0.0105), and **cosd** ranks 6th in SHAP (1.33) versus 11th in importance (0.0083), while **com** ranks 4th in importance (0.095) but 9th in SHAP (0.650). This implies the model relies heavily on **pd** and **pv** as primary drivers, while features like **via** and **cosd** contribute meaningfully through interactions or non-linear effects that raw split-based importance underrepresents.

[Design Guidance] The strong SHAP association of **pd** (13.61) and **pv** (12.00) with the dielectric target, combined with the secondary role of **blm** (2.73), suggests a hypothesis that compositions balancing packing/volume descriptors with B-site electronegativity may correlate with favorable dielectric behavior. This is a model association only—it does not establish that tuning **pd**, **pv**, or **blm** will causally enhance the dielectric property, and experimental validation would be required.
