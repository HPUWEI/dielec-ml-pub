[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61), `pv` (12.00), and `blm` (2.73), which likely correspond to dielectric polarizability/dipole-related descriptors, polarization-volume or ionic-volume terms, and B-site electronegativity (Matyonov-Batsanov), respectively. These dominate the model's predictions, with `pd` and `pv` together accounting for the largest share of SHAP magnitude.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking largely agrees with the raw importance ranking at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm`), but diverges for `via` (SHAP rank 4, importance rank 10 at 0.010478) and `cosd` (SHAP rank 6, importance rank 11 at 0.008339), while `com` and `sm` rank higher in raw importance (4th and 5th) than in SHAP (9th and 12th). This suggests the model relies on `via` and `cosd` through interactions or context-dependent effects that raw split-based importance understates, whereas `com` and `sm` may contribute more independently but with smaller average marginal impact.

[Design Guidance] The strong association of `pd` and `pv` with the dielectric target suggests that compositions with higher polarizability- and volume-related descriptor values tend to be predicted as higher-performing, but this is a model association and not evidence that tuning these features causally improves dielectric properties. Any design hypothesis should be tested experimentally, since the nested CV spread (R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632, RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395) indicates substantial uncertainty and possible confounding among correlated features.
