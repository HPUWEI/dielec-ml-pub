[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are (pm, vm) at r=0.993 and (vi, vm) at r=0.991, with (vi, pm) close behind at r=0.984. These likely co-vary because pm (A-site/B-site related mass-type descriptor), vm (volume-related descriptor), and vi (ionic/volume-related descriptor) all scale with the size and packing of the perovskite unit cell, so larger ions or expanded lattices shift all three together.

[Redundancy] The cluster {pm, vm, vi, m} shows overlapping statistical information, with pm–m at r=0.982 and vm–m at r=0.96, and the separate pair (pd, va) at r=-0.806 also shares substantial variance. This overlap suggests these features are partially redundant, but removal of any single feature cannot be justified from correlation alone and should be tested via ablation or feature-importance analysis.

[Design Implication] Given this structure, a compact informative set would pair one representative from the {pm, vm, vi, m} cluster (e.g., vm) with one from the (pd, va) pair (e.g., pd), then add chemically distinct descriptors such as the electronegativity/ionization features (blm, blsd, Em, Ev, Esd) and GII to capture dielectric behavior beyond size and volume effects.
