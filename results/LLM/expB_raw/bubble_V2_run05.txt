[Strong Correlations] The two most strongly correlated pairs are (pm, vm) with r=0.993 and (vi, vm) with r=0.991, indicating that perovskite A-site/B-site mass and ionic volume descriptors co-vary almost perfectly in this dataset. This likely reflects that heavier constituent ions tend to occupy larger ionic volumes in the same crystallographic sites, so mass and volume are not independent chemical descriptors here.

[Redundancy] The features pm, vm, vi, and m form a highly overlapping cluster (pm–vm r=0.993, vi–vm r=0.991, vi–pm r=0.984, pm–m r=0.982, vm–m r=0.96), and pd–va is strongly negatively correlated (r=-0.806); these features carry substantially shared statistical information. However, correlation alone does not establish that any feature can definitely be removed, since nonlinear or interaction effects in the Gradient Boosting model may still use each one.

[Design Implication] For predicting the dielectric property, a compact informative set could combine one representative from the pm/vm/vi/m cluster (e.g., vm or pm) with pd and va to capture the strong negative pd–va relationship, while retaining chemically distinct descriptors such as the electronegativity/ionization-energy features (blm, blsd, Em, Ev, Esd) and GII. This avoids over-weighting the nearly collinear mass–volume information while preserving complementary A-site, B-site, and structural/electronic signals.
