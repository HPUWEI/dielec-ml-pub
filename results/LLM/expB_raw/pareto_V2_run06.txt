[Dominance] Six features account for 80% of predictive importance: pd (0.312094), pv (0.178505), blm (0.151432), com (0.095113), sm (0.058921), and Em (0.048412). Importance is concentrated, with pd and pv alone contributing ~49% and the top three (pd, pv, blm) contributing ~64%.

[Physical Meaning] pd and pv likely encode packing-density and volume-related descriptors of the perovskite lattice, which govern polarizability and ionic displacement under an applied field, directly influencing dielectric response. blm, a B-site electronegativity descriptor (Matyonov-Batsanov), affects B–O bond covalency and hence the electronic contribution to polarizability, while com and sm capture compositional and symmetry/structural motifs that modulate dipole ordering.

[Simplification] The long-tail features with importances below ~0.01—including asd (0.00096), pm (0.000758), GII (0.001393), ssd (0.00167), Esd (0.003693), d (0.004818), vm (0.005425), blsd (0.005798), devvm (0.005865), va (0.006201), bvsdm (0.00726), lsd (0.007849), vi (0.008227), and cosd (0.008339)—are candidates for further investigation as lower-priority variables; whether they can be removed without degrading performance requires a separate retraining/ablation experiment.
