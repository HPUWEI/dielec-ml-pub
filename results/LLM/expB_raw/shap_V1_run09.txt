[Key Features] The three most important features by SHAP mean |value| are pd (13.612782), pv (11.997661), and blm (2.731782), which likely reflect packing density, pore volume, and B-site electronegativity–Matyonov-Batsanov, respectively, all of which can influence polarization and dielectric response in perovskite-like materials. The large gap between pd/pv and blm suggests that structural/volumetric descriptors dominate the model's predictions over compositional electronegativity terms.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking largely agrees with the raw importance ranking at the top (pd, pv, blm), but via rises to 4th in SHAP (1.885943) despite ranking 10th in raw importance (0.010478), while com drops from 4th in raw importance (0.095113) to 9th in SHAP (0.649655). This implies the model relies on via through interactions or nonlinear effects that raw split-based importance underrepresents, whereas com's high raw importance may be partly redundant with pd/pv or less consistently impactful across samples.

[Design Guidance] Because pd and pv dominate both rankings, compositions or processing routes that systematically shift packing density and pore volume are associated with the largest predicted changes in dielectric properties, but this is a model association and not evidence that tuning pd or pv alone will causally enhance the target. Any design hypothesis should therefore treat pd/pv as screening variables to be validated experimentally alongside blm and via, rather than as direct levers for property optimization.
