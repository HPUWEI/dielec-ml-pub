[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are `pd` (13.61), `pv` (12.00), and `blm` (2.73), with `pd` and `pv` dominating by roughly 5–10× over the next tier. In dielectric materials, `pd` and `pv` likely encode polarization-related descriptors (e.g., polarizability/dipole-volume terms), while `blm` plausibly reflects B-site bond-length/mean bonding character influencing polarizability and lattice stiffness.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (`pd`, `pv`, `blm`), but diverge lower down: `via` ranks 4th in SHAP (1.89) yet only 10th in raw importance (0.0105), and `cosd` ranks 6th in SHAP (1.33) versus 11th in raw importance (0.0083), whereas `com` is 4th in raw importance (0.0951) but 9th in SHAP (0.65). This suggests the Gradient Boosting model relies on `via` and `cosd` through interactions or context-dependent splits that raw split-gain importance understates, while `com`'s high raw importance may partly reflect redundant variance shared with correlated features.

[Design Guidance] A cautious hypothesis is that compositions simultaneously favoring high `pd`/`pv` and moderate `blm` may associate with the dielectric property in this dataset, but this is a model association, not evidence of causality—`via` and `cosd`'s elevated SHAP values further indicate that any such relationship is likely conditional on other features rather than a simple monotonic lever.
