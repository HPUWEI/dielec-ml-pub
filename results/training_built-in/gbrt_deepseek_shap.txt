[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are **pd** (packing fraction, 13.61), **pv** (polarizability per unit volume, 12.00), and **blm** (average bond length, 2.73). In dielectric materials, **pd** and **pv** plausibly reflect how densely polarizable units are packed (governing the density of dipolar/ionic response per volume), while **blm** relates to bond geometry and the ease of bond-level polarization or lattice distortion under an electric field.

[Model Insight] SHAP and raw importance largely agree at the top (**pd** 13.61 vs 0.312; **pv** 12.00 vs 0.179; **blm** 2.73 vs 0.151), but they diverge for **via** (SHAP 1.89, importance 0.010) and **cosd** (SHAP 1.33, importance 0.008), which rank far higher by SHAP than by raw importance, while **com** (SHAP 0.65, importance 0.095) and **sm** (SHAP 0.45, importance 0.059) rank higher by raw importance. This suggests the model's split-based importance is concentrated in **pd**/**pv**/**blm**, whereas **via** and **cosd** carry consistent directional contributions (possibly through interactions or nonlinear context) that raw importance understates.

[Design Guidance] The model associates high **pd** and **pv** with the dielectric target, and the 80%-importance set (**pd, pv, blm, com, sm, Em**) clusters around packing, polarizability, and bond energetics—so compositions or structures that jointly raise polarizability per volume while maintaining dense packing are worth screening. This is a model association, not evidence of causality: changing **pd** or **pv** may not causally improve the dielectric property, and the wide CV spread (R²=0.7472 ± 0.1632, RMSE=15.4844 ± 6.7395) warrants validation before any design claim.
