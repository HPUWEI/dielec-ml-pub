[Key Features] The top features by SHAP mean |value| are ppv (1.161865), bg (0.825562), and bpv (0.603609), representing polarizability per unit volume, band gap, and bond density per unit cell volume respectively. These correspond to the intrinsic polarization capacity, electronic polarization/loss behavior, and structural rigidity of the dielectric material.

[Model Insight] The SHAP ranking and raw feature importance ranking are largely consistent, with ppv, bg, and bpv occupying the top three positions in both; however, blm (SHAP: 0.441251) and d (SHAP: 0.440295) are nearly tied in SHAP while d (0.138241) ranks above blm (0.131618) in raw importance, suggesting the model may capture subtle interaction effects or nonlinear dependencies involving bond length that raw split-based importance underrepresents.

[Design Guidance] The model associates higher ppv and bpv, alongside moderate bg, with the target dielectric property, suggesting that compositions balancing high polarizability per volume with a sufficiently wide band gap and dense bonding framework may be worth prioritizing for screening. However, this is a model-based association observed in the training data and does not establish that tuning these features will causally improve dielectric performance, as unmeasured confounders and synthesis constraints may govern the true relationship.
