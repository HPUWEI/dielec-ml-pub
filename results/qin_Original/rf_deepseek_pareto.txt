[Dominance] Five of the six features account for 80% of predictive importance: ppv (0.309568), bg (0.190100), bpv (0.145877), d (0.138241), and blm (0.131618). [Physical Meaning] ppv (polarizability per unit volume) is the most direct intrinsic driver of dielectric constant, so its top rank is physically consistent with the target. bg (band gap) governs electronic polarization and dielectric loss, with wider gaps generally suppressing loss contributions. bpv (bond density) reflects structural rigidity and cross-linking, which constrains polarizability and thus modulates permittivity.
